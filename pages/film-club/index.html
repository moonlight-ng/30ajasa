Film Club

Community Cinema Screenings

The Film Club at 30 Ajasa Street brings together cinema enthusiasts for regular screenings, discussions, and workshops. Our space hosts both classic and contemporary films, with a focus on African cinema and independent filmmakers.

Join us for weekly screenings every Friday evening, where we explore diverse voices in cinema and foster meaningful conversations about film as art and cultural expression.
